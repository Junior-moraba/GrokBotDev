## What it does

Point it at an ad account and it hunts for money leaking out: campaigns, ad sets and single creatives that keep spending while converting nobody, plus landing pages that lose the visitors. Each finding carries a dollar estimate of what fixing it is worth. Nothing pauses without your say-so, and access is read-only. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
