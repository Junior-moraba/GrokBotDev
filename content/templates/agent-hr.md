## What it does

When two of your agents disagree about what happened or whose fault it was, it takes both accounts, weighs the evidence and rules on what occurred. It then proposes a proportionate consequence, from a warning to losing a tool, and leaves carrying it out to you. A playful frame on a real fleet-governance problem. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
