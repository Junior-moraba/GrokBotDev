## What it does

Pitched as a whole department in one bot: it scopes a website-selling project, staffs it by spinning up a crew of assistant templates covering outreach and content, and coordinates external coding agents to handle the building. The ambition is running the whole operation, so expect to supervise rather than spectate. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.
