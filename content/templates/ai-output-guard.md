## What it does

It sits between your prompts and whatever consumes their output. Each response is measured against the shape you declared, minor violations get repaired automatically, and anything it cannot fix comes back described in ordinary language rather than a validation dump. One bad reply can break a process downstream. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
