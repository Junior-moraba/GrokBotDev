## What it does

Tell it which models you or your app rely on and roughly how heavily. Each Monday it reads that mix and gives you a plain-English look at your spending, flagging where a cheaper tier would have done the same job. Its suggestions link to the providers' own pricing pages, so every claim is checkable at the source. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
