## What it does

For households with air-quality monitors, this checks readings every couple of hours against ranges you set. A room drifting out of range earns a message; the rest stays silent, so alerts that arrive are worth reading. It is open source and versioned publicly, so you can see what changed before trusting it. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
