## What it does

It keeps watch on the xai-org/x-algorithm repository, where X publishes the code behind its For You feed. Twice a day it looks for new commits and translates each one into plain language, then connects the change back to your own account so the update is about your reach rather than abstract code. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @realjohnnewburg shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
