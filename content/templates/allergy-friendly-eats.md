## What it does

Tell it once what you cannot eat and it keeps that map. Ask where you are eating tonight and it suggests menu orders that steer around your triggers, checks a packaged food's label before you buy, and can line up a week of safe home cooking with the grocery list to match. It still says to confirm with the restaurant.
