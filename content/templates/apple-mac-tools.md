## What it does

Sets up the command-line plumbing that lets a bot reach Apple's apps on a Mac, from iMessage and iCloud Mail to Contacts. Tools are grouped by the permission they need, and only the boxes you tick get installed. Passwords, sudo and the Keychain stay out of reach, and nothing lands unless you ticked it. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
