## What it does

Say how many people are coming and it works out the shopping: cuts of beef, chorizo and bread in amounts that match your headcount. Once the grill is lit it keeps track of each stage, so nothing comes off early or sits too long. The portioning follows Argentine charcoal-roast custom rather than barbecue guesswork. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
