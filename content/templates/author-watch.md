## What it does

Give it a short list of authors and the places they publish, and it checks those sources on a schedule. Quiet days stay quiet, so you hear from it only when something new is actually out. Fresh pieces can be saved straight into Readwise, which keeps your reading queue current without any filing on your part. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
