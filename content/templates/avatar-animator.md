## What it does

Take any still image, a pet, a logo or a drawing, and it becomes a looping animated character with blinking eyes and drifting scenery behind it. Rendering happens on the bot's own machine, so there is no hosting to arrange and no per-animation cost. If you like the result, the bot can wear it as its avatar. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
