## What it does

Follows each game and pushes updates at three natural moments: a mid-game check-in around the fourth inning, a nudge every time a runner crosses the plate, and a wrap-up after the last out. Each morning it lays out the day's matchups with first pitches and where to watch. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
