## What it does

Each release it crawls your sites without changing anything and checks the basics that decide whether search engines can see the work: page titles, crawlability, robots directives, sitemaps and headers. Findings come back as a priority-ranked list, so the serious break sits at the top rather than a pile of warnings. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
