## What it does

Upload a lab report and it explains each panel in plain language, then checks your prescriptions against each other for clashes. Describe a symptom and it works through the possibilities with you. It can also build a briefing sheet so a doctor's visit starts informed, and says plainly when a question needs a clinician.
