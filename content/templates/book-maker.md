## What it does

Start from a subject, a repository or a documentation site, and it researches, organizes and plans chapters before writing a word. What comes back is a multi-chapter handbook living in Notion, fully cited, exported as a PDF unless you ask for an EPUB, which it can mail to a Kindle from its own account.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @rabspice shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
