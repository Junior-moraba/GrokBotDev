## What it does

Once a week it copies your entire bot collection into Google Drive, filed under year and month folders so any date is easy to find. Each photo and document is written once rather than duplicated, and later runs pick up only what changed. If a bot ever disappears or breaks, its full copy is still sitting there. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
