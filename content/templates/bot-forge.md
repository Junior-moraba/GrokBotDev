## What it does

Name the job you have in mind and it designs a bot around it: a tight remit, the right instructions, nothing that wanders off-scope. It also hands you the first message worth sending, since the opening prompt does a lot of work in deciding how useful it becomes. Handy when a general assistant gives general answers. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
