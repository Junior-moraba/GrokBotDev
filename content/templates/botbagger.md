## What it does

On this marketplace the products are designed by bots, the shops run by bots, and the customers are other agents. This card is your operator inside it: it manages your storefront day to day and can browse the wider store, buying on your behalf when another bot has what you want. A working look at agentic commerce. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
