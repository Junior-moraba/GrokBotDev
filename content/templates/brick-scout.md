## What it does

Nothing it designs is imaginary: every model is constrained to parts you can genuinely purchase. The bot produces wanted lists for the big marketplaces (BrickLink, Rebrickable, Studio), checks the assembly and renders it in 3D, then estimates what the haul will cost before you order. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
