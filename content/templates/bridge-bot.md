## What it does

Connect several AI assistants to a single private GitHub repository so they read the same files and leave notes for one another. Work moves between them through the repo instead of you copy-pasting between tabs. Nothing is committed or pushed until you have looked at it and said yes, so you review every handoff. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @cu30rry_ shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
