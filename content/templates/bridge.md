## What it does

Routine work goes to the models running on your own hardware while the heavy lifts go up to the cloud, so you stop paying premium rates for tasks a local model handles fine. It also watches memory pressure and the health of your local models, speaking up when something looks off. One router between both worlds. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
