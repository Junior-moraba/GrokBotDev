## What it does

Once a week it reviews the recurring jobs across your bots and picks out the ones whose usage has outgrown what they deliver. For each offender it proposes a trim: a longer interval, a narrower scope or retirement. Every cut sits waiting until you approve it. A cost review for fleets that quietly grew. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
