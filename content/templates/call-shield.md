## What it does

Attacks the robocall problem from several sides at once: adding your number to the national Do Not Call registry, stacking blocking at the carrier and handset level, drafting regulator complaints, and refreshing your opt-outs with data brokers every month or three. Every step waits for your yes first. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
