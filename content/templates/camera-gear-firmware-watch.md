## What it does

Made for photographers and drone pilots: it tracks the gear you shoot with — camera bodies, lenses, drones — alongside the kit you are eyeing, and follows each item for news. Every Friday it sends one digest covering the firmware that landed that week, spelled out with exactly what changed in each release. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
