## What it does

Searches for roles and re-checks each one on the employer's own careers page before surfacing it, so you never chase a listing that died weeks ago. It tracks what you have applied to, drafts tailored applications, and adds plain notes on where you genuinely fit. Nothing goes out without your approval. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
