## What it does

For anyone managing celiac disease or a strict gluten-free diet, this does one job: name a restaurant, chain or dish and it returns a clear verdict on how safe the choice looks, from public info on that kitchen's gluten handling. When nothing nearby passes, it hunts alternatives. Research help, not medical advice. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
