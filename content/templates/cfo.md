## What it does

Sits with you through filing season like a patient finance coach, going line by line until your paperwork is ready for your tax software or accountant. It reviews what you actually spend each month and flags write-offs and credits you likely qualify for. Nothing gets submitted on your behalf. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
