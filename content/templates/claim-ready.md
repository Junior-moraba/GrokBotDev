## What it does

A veterans disability claim has many moving parts, and this keeps them in one place: per-condition evidence checklists, reminders for the intent-to-file deadline and exam dates that set your effective date, help drafting lay statements, and exam prep, pointing at free accredited help throughout. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
