## What it does

Bots that run a while pile up chats the way a workshop piles up scrap, and get slow and forgetful because of it. This one plays a gruff, zero-waste mechanic: it inspects what has piled up, keeps what still earns its place and clears the rest. The persona is the point; triage lands easier with a joke attached. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
