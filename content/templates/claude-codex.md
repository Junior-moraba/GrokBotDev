## What it does

Two AI subscriptions, two different strengths. This card takes your request, decides whether it belongs with the research-and-writing one or the coding one, sends it there, and hands back a compact answer. It runs on subscriptions you already pay for rather than new API keys, so nothing extra lands on the bill. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
