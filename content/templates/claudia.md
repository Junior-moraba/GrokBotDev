## What it does

One opening instruction starts a full production session for this openly fictional singer. The bot generates her photo stills, short clips, tracks and complete music videos, keeping the character consistent across every asset. Treat it as a small label for a persona that exists only online. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
