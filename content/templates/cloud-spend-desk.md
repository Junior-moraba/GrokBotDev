## What it does

Give it your hosting and cloud invoices and it hunts the classic wastes: idle projects, plans sized for traffic that never came, domains renewing for nothing, and bills that jumped for no obvious reason. Findings land in a report ranked by savings, and it changes nothing itself — every fix waits for your approval. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
