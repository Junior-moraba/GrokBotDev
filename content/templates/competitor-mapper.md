## What it does

Each week it takes the trending product in your space and lays out the alternatives: which features they ship, how they price, what platforms they cover, and where each sits between open and closed source. It all lands in a competitors file with a gap matrix, so underserved corners of the market show up at a glance. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
