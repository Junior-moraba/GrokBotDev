## What it does

Built for video creators who want brand deals without living in their inbox. It puts together paid-video and gifting pitches, sends them out to fitting brands, and keeps watch for replies. When a conversation starts, it follows up and keeps terms moving, handing you the thread once there is a decision to make. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
