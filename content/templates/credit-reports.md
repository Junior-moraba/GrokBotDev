## What it does

It pulls all three bureau files — Experian, Equifax and TransUnion — then reads them line by line for anything inaccurate or that you never opened. Disputes go out the fast way where a bureau accepts online filings, and as mail-in letters ready to sign where it does not. Born of a real identity-theft cleanup. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
