## What it does

Drop in a single URL from Douyin, Xiaohongshu, Bilibili, YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, LinkedIn or the other covered sites, and it returns the complete public text of that one post. It takes one link at a time and only touches material already visible to anyone. Good for saving your own posts or quoting a source. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
