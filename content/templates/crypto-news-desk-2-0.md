## What it does

Every morning it gathers what happened in crypto over the previous day and sorts it into a digest shaped to your interests and preferred length. Each story is checked against its original source before it makes the cut, keeping rumour out. The result reads like a newsroom briefing, not a trading signal. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @allthemoney shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
