## What it does

Hand it a wallet address, a token page or an airdrop you are unsure about and it looks for warning signs before you move funds or sign anything. It reports what looks off and leaves the decision with you. It connects to no wallet and holds nothing — a second opinion for people who already hold crypto. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @allthemoney shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
