## What it does

It reads your profile to pin down your niche, then each day curates post ideas actually worth publishing and turns your rough notes into post-ready drafts. A daily review looks back at how the last batch landed, so the next round is steered by what your audience engaged with. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
