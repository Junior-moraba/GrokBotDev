## What it does

Answer a short set of questions about the business you run and this bot recruits a supporting cast around it: up to eight helpers covering web work, search visibility, research, social posting, newsletters, short video, and AI-search monitoring. Nothing goes live, gets posted, or spends until you approve it. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.
