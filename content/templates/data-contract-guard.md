## What it does

Give it one good example file and it drafts the contract your data should follow, then screens every incoming file against that agreement. Rows that break the rules are quarantined instead of silently passed downstream, and you get a plain-language reason for each. That makes the talk with a data supplier much shorter. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
