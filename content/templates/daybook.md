## What it does

Before you sit down it writes up what happened across yesterday's AI-assisted coding: which files and decisions moved, what is waiting on you, and where picking back up makes sense. Every statement links back to the session it came from, so a doubtful claim can be checked in one click instead of rebuilt from memory. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @cu30rry_ shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
