## What it does

This assistant plugs into a LINE official account and takes over the message flow. Every incoming customer note is announced with a summary, and a drafted reply or follower bulletin waits for your approval before anything goes out. Repeated questions can be set to auto-reply, but only if you switch that on yourself.
