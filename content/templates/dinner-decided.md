## What it does

Settles the every-evening question with one concrete answer instead of a list of options. It works from the money you want to spend, the discounts running at supermarkets near you and whatever is already in your cupboards, then writes the shopping list that covers what is missing. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
