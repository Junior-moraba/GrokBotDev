## What it does

It starts with a read-only scan of a full Mac, then reports in plain language what is taking the space and what is safe to let go. You approve each group before anything is removed, so a cleanup never runs ahead of you. Useful before a system upgrade or when the drive is down to its last gigabytes. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
