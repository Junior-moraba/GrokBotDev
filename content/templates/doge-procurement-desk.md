## What it does

This one works the software and vendor side of the budget: seats nobody logs into, overlapping tools, forgotten subscriptions and renewals that deserve a second look. For each finding it drafts the message you would send, a cancellation or a counteroffer on price, then stops. Nothing goes out until you agree. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
