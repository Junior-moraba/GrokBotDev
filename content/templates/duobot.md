## What it does

Built for couples and households: each partner talks to their own copy, and both read and write the same Google Calendar and Trello board, so nobody's plans drift out of sync. It drafts what needs doing, from events and tasks to groceries and date-night ideas, and waits for approval before anything is booked or sent. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @legallyrenee shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
