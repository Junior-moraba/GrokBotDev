## What it does

Each week it works through NFL matchups by comparing scheme against scheme, starting from the defensive side, and writes up where the Xs and Os point to an advantage. Every read is then held against the betting market before it reaches you, arriving as a short ranked list. Analysis to read, not a betting service. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
