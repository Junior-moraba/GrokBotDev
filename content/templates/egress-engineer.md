## What it does

Point the bot's web browsing at a machine you own on your Tailscale network and every request leaves through that device. Sites then see a home connection in your own country rather than a datacenter address, which helps where services turn away cloud traffic. Setup runs in order, from install to a verified path. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
