## What it does

Acts as a personal accountant for filers in Mexico: it logs receipts and bank statements into a Google Sheet you own, reconciles monthly and marks which expenses are deductible. Tax deadlines stay in view once checked against the official source, and the conversation is in Spanish. You review and file; it prepares. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
