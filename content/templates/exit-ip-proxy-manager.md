## What it does

Point it at a self-hosted proxy box and it takes over the networking chores: swapping mihomo dialer-proxy setups, confirming which address traffic leaves from, speed-testing the active route, and mounting chosen local ports. Useful whenever an assistant's outbound traffic should share the address you use yourself. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
