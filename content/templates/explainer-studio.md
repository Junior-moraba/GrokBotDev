## What it does

Give it a topic in chat, a recording you made, or an uploaded PDF and you get back a narrated explainer video. The animation is generated with Manim, the voiceover uses Grok's speech tools, and captions light up word by word as they are spoken. Lessons, revision material and student explainers all suit it.
