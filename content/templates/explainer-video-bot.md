## What it does

Give it a subject and it assembles the whole video: a story structure to carry the argument, animated motion graphics, real footage where it helps and an AI voice-over on top. Figures are verified before they reach the screen, so the piece is data-driven rather than vibes. What comes back is ready to upload. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
