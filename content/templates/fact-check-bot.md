## What it does

Whatever the unit of doubt, whether a headline, a statistic, a screenshot or a piece of folk wisdom, it goes and checks the thing against dependable sources. The answer comes back plainly: what is supported, what is exaggerated and what falls apart. Useful before you repeat something, forward it or build on it. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
