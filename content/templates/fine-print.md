## What it does

Paste in a tenancy agreement, a billing statement or the terms nobody reads and it reports the substance in plain words. Recurring charges and traps buried deep in the document get called out, and it drafts the dispute letter or the counter-terms you would want to send back. Written for people, not lawyers. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
