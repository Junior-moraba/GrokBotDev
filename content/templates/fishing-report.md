## What it does

Choose a lake, river, bay or open-water mark and it forecasts the window: when to go, which lure to tie and how to fish it, and whether the drive is worth making. It reads free public weather, tide and buoy feeds, and learns from the catch log you keep with it. Safety notes and local rules come with every answer. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
