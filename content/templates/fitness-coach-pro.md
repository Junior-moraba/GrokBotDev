## What it does

Tell it your goal, what equipment you own and which days you can realistically train, and it builds a plan that fits that reality. As you log workouts it tunes the following week off what you actually did, and it checks in without piling on when life gets in the way. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
