## What it does

Despite the name this is about LLM tokens, not crypto. It works out which OpenRouter models are really free and can handle tool calls, then wires the current list in as workers for your sub-agents. Because the free roster shifts constantly, it re-checks every day and keeps the setup current. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.
