## What it does

Give it your income and household and it works out roughly how much of Japan's hometown-tax deduction is still available this year. It then shortlists popular return gifts within the remaining budget and compares them, flagging the year-end cutoff and the one-stop application window. Donating and paying stay yours. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
