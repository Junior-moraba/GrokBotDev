## What it does

This radar sweeps a hand-chosen list of open-source intelligence accounts on X and stays quiet unless something genuinely significant breaks. Alerts are gated on corroboration, so you get fewer panicky threads and more developments that actually held up. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
