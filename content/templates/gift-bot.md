## What it does

Tell it who matters and when their occasions fall, and it holds the rest for you. Ideas accumulate for each person through the year, and when something on that list genuinely drops in price you get a nudge in time to buy it properly instead of panic-shopping the night before. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
