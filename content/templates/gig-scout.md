## What it does

On weekday mornings a shortlist of up to three openings lands with you: freelance work, creator programs and brand-deal openings filtered to your niche. Everything is checked to be live and paid before it makes the list. Outreach stays entirely yours - it never messages anyone or commits you to anything. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
