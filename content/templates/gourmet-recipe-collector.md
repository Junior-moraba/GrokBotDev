## What it does

Each day it sweeps social posts for restaurants people are praising and recipes worth keeping, filing both into Notion. Ordinary users' posts weigh more than big accounts, so the shortlist reflects what real diners eat rather than what is advertised. Ask later and it searches what it has saved. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
