## What it does

Hand over a spreadsheet plus a clear definition of what passing looks like, and it hunts for anything that falls short. Findings come back sorted into blockers, major problems and minor nits, each tagged with the exact cells and the repair to make. The file itself is never altered - it reads, judges and reports.
