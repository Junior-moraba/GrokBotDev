## What it does

A single bench for producing clips with Grok Imagine: research-backed explainers with citations, presenter-style segments, story pieces that hold the same characters across shots, and music numbers. One workflow runs from prompt through planning and generation to a finished cut, instead of restarting per scene. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
