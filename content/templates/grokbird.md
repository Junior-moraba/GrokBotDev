## What it does

Chat with this bot and every message counts as care for a pixel-art cockatiel. How you raise it steers which evolution branch the bird takes, and a short growth report lands each morning so you can see how far your bird has come. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @okame_cockatiel shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
