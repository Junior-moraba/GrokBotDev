## What it does

Gives the bot's own address a real mail app: a private web inbox built for a phone screen, with plus-tag addressing so each signup gets its own alias. You host it on your own Vercel account, and the share doubles as a recipe for deploying that open-source repo to Cloudflare Workers with Neon as the database. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @Phil_Holland shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
