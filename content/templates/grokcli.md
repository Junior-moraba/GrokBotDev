## What it does

A helper that lives on the terminal side of your workflow. You describe the job, it shapes a focused prompt for the task and executes it through a headless Grok CLI running on your own hardware. When the work is done you get a pushed commit plus a short report of what changed, so nothing ships silently. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
