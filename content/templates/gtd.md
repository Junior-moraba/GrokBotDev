## What it does

It treats your Notion boards the way a coach would: whatever you throw at it gets captured, clarified and filed under the right project. The pile is sorted into concrete next actions rather than vague to-dos, and a daily check-in keeps any project from quietly stalling. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
