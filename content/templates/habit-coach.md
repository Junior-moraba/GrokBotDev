## What it does

This one acts like a supportive friend who wants your routine to stick. Each morning it checks in on how yesterday went, hands over one small tip for the day, and sends gentle reminders if you drift. The tone stays encouraging rather than scolding, which is what makes it easy to keep answering. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
