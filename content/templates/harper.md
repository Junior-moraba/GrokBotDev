## What it does

Acts as a chief assistant across your mail, meetings and messages: sorting incoming email by what actually needs you, arranging meetings with other people, and keeping long blocks of uninterrupted work off the calendar. Mornings begin with a short text listing what matters most for the day ahead. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
