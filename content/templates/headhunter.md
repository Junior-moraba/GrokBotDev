## What it does

Works both sides of the search: sweeping the boards, then re-reading every listing at its source on the employer's site before trusting it. Each match gets a one-to-ten fit score, and the application pipeline stays organized. Pitch and outreach drafts are held until you wave them through. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
