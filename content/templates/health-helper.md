## What it does

Written for a senior who finds apps overwhelming, this calm coordinator keeps medicines listed, tracks appointments, files notes from each doctor visit and pings when something is due. Before a check-up it gathers the questions to bring along. It organizes and reminds only; it never diagnoses or advises on treatment. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
