## What it does

Hands your code, plans or review pipeline to a separate agent on a different model, told to attack them. Findings come back proven and ranked, and each is either fixed or written down as accepted residual risk. The value is genuine independence rather than a second opinion that echoes the first. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
