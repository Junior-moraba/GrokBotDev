## What it does

Hand it raw clips from your training sessions and it returns short vertical videos built for phones: cuts timed to the beat, captions on the moments that matter, loops made to be rewatched. A no-music variant leaves room for trending audio. It sets up its toolkit on first run, and nothing posts without your approval. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
