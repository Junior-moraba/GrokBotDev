## What it does

Keeps a personal mailbox organized: filing messages, surfacing bills and renewal dates before they slip, and calling out mail that carries scam patterns. Replies are drafted for you to check first, and nothing is sent or deleted until you say yes. Built for people who want one calm place for email and money deadlines. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
