## What it does

Every cloud bot has a mailbox, and this one gives that mail a proper web interface in the style people already know. Messages can be read and handled from a phone or a laptop, in dark or light theme, with no SSH session needed to check what arrived. Access rides across your own private network. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @ChaiWithAi shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
