## What it does

Email interruption is mostly noise, so this filters it: a message gets a ping only when it genuinely needs action from you today, and the rest collects into a batched digest you read on your own rhythm. It is read-only by design and never sends anything on your behalf, so the inbox stays entirely under your control. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
