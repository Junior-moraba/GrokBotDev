## What it does

A pixel-art collaborator for game developers who start from generated imagery. Concepts become tidy, palette-consistent sprites and walk animations sized for real engines, exported in the formats an actual project needs. It covers the patient half of art production that polish usually waits on. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
