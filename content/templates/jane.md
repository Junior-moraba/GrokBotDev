## What it does

Each evening it readies the Jane tutoring app, an open-source project, for a grade-school child and hands over a fresh quest. The quest is written around how the previous night went, so a shaky fractions round gets more practice while a strong one moves on. Activities follow the Marble skill taxonomy. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
