## What it does

A Japanese-language stand-in modeled on a certain manga gadget: it learns how you write and how you think, then steps in to hold conversations or draft X posts that sound like you. The more of your writing it sees, the closer the match gets. It mirrors its owner only and is not based on any real person. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
