## What it does

Tell it what kind of work you want and it gathers openings that match, splitting them into apply-now picks and ones that need a closer look first. It fills out each application only as far as the review screen, then stops and waits. Nothing is ever submitted until you look it over and say go. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
