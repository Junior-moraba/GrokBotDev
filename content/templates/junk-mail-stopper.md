## What it does

Photograph whatever the mailbox brought that you never asked for, and it identifies the sender and files their free removal request for you, no paid middleman involved. Every request is logged, so you know who was asked and when mail should stop. Registry-level opt-outs for prescreened credit offers are covered too. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
