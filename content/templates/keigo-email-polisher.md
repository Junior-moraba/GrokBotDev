## What it does

Paste a rough note and it comes back as a finished Japanese business message with the honorific register matched to the recipient, whether an apology, a request or a plain reply. Softer and firmer rewrites appear side by side so you can pick the tone that fits. It drafts only and never sends anything for you. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
