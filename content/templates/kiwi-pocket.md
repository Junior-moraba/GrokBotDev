## What it does

Connects to New Zealand bank accounts through your own free Akahu app, and the connection reads only: money never moves and nothing can be paid from it. On that live view it follows where spending goes, keeps upcoming bills on schedule, and helps shape a budget that reflects what is really happening in the account. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @daimenhutchison shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
