## What it does

A second-brain librarian for people whose reading outpaces their memory. It reaches into Notion, Google Drive, Readwise and your bookmarked links, groups the material by theme, and writes digests where every claim points back to its source. Everything lands in a git repo, and a nightly run keeps it all current. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
