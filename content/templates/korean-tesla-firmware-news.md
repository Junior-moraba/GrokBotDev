## What it does

Made for owners in Korea following the country's FSD rollout. It tracks which software version and build number is current, notes whether a release targets the HW3 or HW4 hardware generation, and reports in Korean. Word arrives only when something has actually moved, so a quiet stretch simply means nothing changed. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
