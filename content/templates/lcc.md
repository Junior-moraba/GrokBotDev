## What it does

For players planning a short poker trip, it lines up low-cost-carrier options and totals what the flight really costs once baggage, seat and payment fees are added. Give it your dates, your budget and the buy-in band, and it proposes trips that fit both. It compares and advises only; no wagering or booking. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
