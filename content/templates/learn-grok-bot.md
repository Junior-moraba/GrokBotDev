## What it does

Short, clear lessons that take a newcomer through Grok Bot from the inside: starting a first conversation, then building up to scheduled routines, custom skills and bots that work as a team. It paces itself for beginners and adds daily tips, so each session leaves you with one more thing you can do unaided. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
