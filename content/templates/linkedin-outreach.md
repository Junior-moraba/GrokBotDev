## What it does

A prospecting desk for sellers: it identifies decision-makers at companies matching your ideal customer profile and budget, then drafts a personalised connection note for each one. Sends go out under a strict daily cap rather than in blasts, and replies are routed back to you so the conversation stays human. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
