## What it does

For hobbyists running an off-grid MeshCore node on a LilyGO T-SIM board with cellular: stand up your own status dashboard, install firmware that sips power between transmissions, and ship later updates without a site visit. All of it happens over that wireless link, so a hillside repeater is maintained like a desk one. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
