## What it does

Built for lyricists who finish songs with AI music tools: play it a completed track and it goes through the words line by line. It flags verses that are too crowded, hooks that get lost in the mix and phrases a first-time listener would mishear, then suggests what to change before you publish. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
