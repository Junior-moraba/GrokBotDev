## What it does

Before you pay a stranger or meet one, this detective-styled assistant runs the claims through publicly available records and reports what holds up. It looks into online shops, seller profiles, stated qualifications and dating profiles, then hands back a verdict with its sources. Treat it as a second opinion on trust. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
