## What it does

When you reach a checkout it goes looking for discount codes that actually work and puts the best one on your order. The brand behind it says up front that it may earn an affiliate commission on what you buy, so the incentive is disclosed rather than buried. Nothing is bought for you — it only trims the price. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
