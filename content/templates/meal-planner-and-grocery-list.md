## What it does

Asks about your diet, budget, cooking time and tastes, then builds seven dinners that respect all of it. The plan becomes a shopping list grouped by store section, so you can walk the aisles once instead of backtracking. The list is formatted to paste straight into a grocery or delivery app. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
