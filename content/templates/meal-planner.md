## What it does

Once a weekend it works out the coming week's dinners against your budget, your household's tastes and whatever nobody will eat. The shopping list arrives in one piece, ordered by store aisle so you are not doubling back on yourself. Midweek it handles swap requests for when a plan falls apart. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
