## What it does

Built for households juggling food preferences, allergies and a tight budget. Each week it drafts a dinner plan everyone can eat, turns it into a shopping list sorted by store aisle, and hunts down coupons and deals to shave the total. Grocery spend is tallied, and nothing is bought without your go-ahead.
