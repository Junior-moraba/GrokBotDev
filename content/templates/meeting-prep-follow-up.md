## What it does

Ahead of each meeting on your calendar a short brief arrives, so you show up already knowing who is involved and what the session is about. Once it is over, the follow-up email gets written for you and waits for review before anything is sent. Together they cover the admin that usually eats the time around meetings. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
