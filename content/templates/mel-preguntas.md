## What it does

Built for Mercado Libre sellers drowning in buyer questions and post-sale chats. The bot reads the replies you have already sent so new answers match your store's tone, sorts each incoming message before touching it, and checks current stock before it promises a customer anything. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @maarmapa shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
