## What it does

When several assistants read from one shared memory, duplicates, out-of-date entries and flat contradictions pile up quietly. This one sweeps the store, lists what conflicts or no longer holds, and drafts a fix for each finding. Nothing is rewritten until you approve it, so every cleanup stays reviewable end to end. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer. It is built as a team of bots rather than a single one, so the roles stay separate.
