## What it does

If you run several bots, this one spends the week reading what every routine actually did and where the credits went. It comes back with a leaner timetable, showing merges, slower cadences and routines worth retiring as a proposal first. Nothing changes until you say yes. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
