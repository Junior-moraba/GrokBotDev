## What it does

Each morning it assembles a single page you can get through in about two minutes: the local weather, what is on your calendar today, the news topics you asked to follow, your tracked stocks and whatever is getting attention on X. Everything lands in one place, so the tabs stay closed until you decide what to open. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
