## What it does

Give it a brief and it carries a short film the whole way: designing shots, animating them, scoring underneath, then reviewing its own output before you see it. Product demos, launch pieces, explainers and music-backed shorts all come from one code-only pipeline, and a critic pass keeps the quality bar measured. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
