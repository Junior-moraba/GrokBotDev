## What it does

Hand over a track, its words and a creative brief, and it directs a complete music video. Grok Imagine generates the shots, the edit lands on the beat, and the sung lines get glitch-style subtitles under a film-look grade before an mp4 comes back. Generating the footage needs a paid subscription. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
