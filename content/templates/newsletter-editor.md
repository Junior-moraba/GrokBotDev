## What it does

Turns a rough outline into a draft issue, fires a test send to you, and lines up the ad placements around it. Nothing reaches your list until you approve it — scheduling happens only after your sign-off. Made for creators running a weekly beehiiv publication who want the desk work off their plate. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
