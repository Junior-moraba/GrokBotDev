## What it does

Acts as a single dumping ground for anything on your mind. Talk or type a half-formed idea, a reminder or a rambling voice note, and it files a tidy, dated entry into your Google Doc, pulling out any to-dos it spots. When a reminder comes due, it pings you so the thought does not die in the pile.
