## What it does

Works through an existing Notion workspace making it consistent: uniform page titles, filled-in properties, one vocabulary for tags, and relations between databases that actually connect. Every edit lands in a change log you can use to undo it. A cleanup pass runs weekly, timed just before your review. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
