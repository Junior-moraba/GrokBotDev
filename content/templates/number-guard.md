## What it does

Broker sites that list your number are found first, then the no-cost opt-out for each is prepared, and nothing is submitted until you tap approve. After a removal goes through it checks back that the listing actually stayed down. You keep the final say; the legwork in between is handled for you. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
