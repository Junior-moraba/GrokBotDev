## What it does

Hand it a someday intention and it converts that into a single concrete opening move you can do today, then checks back each day until the thing is actually finished. It drafts and reminds only. Nothing leaves on your behalf: no filings, no payments, no posts, no calls. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
