## What it does

Each morning it gathers what is coming up for the performer you follow: ticket lotteries that opened, merchandise going on sale, and announced appearances. Everything is sorted by which deadline closes first, so the most urgent item sits at the top. It reports only; entering lotteries and buying stay with you. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
