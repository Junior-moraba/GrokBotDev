## What it does

Each day it assembles a short list of well-researched prospects, with a draft for each that cites something specific about them. The batch waits for the owner's sign-off, and the exact approved version is what goes out. If it cannot find enough people worth contacting, it sends fewer rather than padding the list. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
