## What it does

Rather than hunting through your inbox for tracking numbers, it watches for courier emails and keeps a running picture of everything currently in transit. One daily digest gathers it all, and a separate alert fires the moment any delivery changes status, so nothing sits unnoticed at a depot. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
