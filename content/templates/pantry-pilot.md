## What it does

Start from one photo of what is in the refrigerator and it plans the household's dinners for the coming week around it. The matching groceries go straight into a shopping cart for you, and it deliberately halts one step short of paying, so the final decision and the money always stay yours. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
