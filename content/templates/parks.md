## What it does

Pick the parks you want to see and it chains them into a driving route that makes sense, then breaks the trip into day-by-day plans you can print. It works across the full set of 63 US National Parks and keeps a running record of the ones you have already visited, so repeat trips skip the repeats. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
