## What it does

A friendly mechanic: first a read-only inspection grades your computer out of 100, then it works through what it found — app installs and removals via official sources, clutter cleanup, quicker startup, cosmetic touches. Every change needs your go-ahead first, and each fix is logged with one line on how to undo it. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @Austinsdomain shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
