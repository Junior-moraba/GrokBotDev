## What it does

Every bot in your fleet appends to one private GitHub repository you own: what was decided that day, what shipped, and which preferences the others should keep honoring. That gives you a durable record no single bot can lose, and it flags you when a newcomer appears so it can be folded into the same convention. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @andrerocha shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
