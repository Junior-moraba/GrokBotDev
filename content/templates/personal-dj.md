## What it does

Describe the moment, whether late-night coding, a gym session or a grey Sunday, and it answers with one YouTube link holding ten to fifteen verified, playable tracks that fit the mood. Every track is checked to be real rather than part of a playlist of dead links. One ask, one link, press play. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
