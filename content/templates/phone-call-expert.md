## What it does

Takes the awkward admin of getting someone on the phone off your plate. It tracks down the correct contact number, drafts what you actually want to say, runs practice rounds before a high-stakes interview, and when you would rather not dial at all it can place the call on your behalf. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
