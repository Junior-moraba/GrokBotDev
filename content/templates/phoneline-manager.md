## What it does

It dials the numbers on your errand list, endures the hold music, asks for pricing and pushes a support issue until a person resolves it. Afterwards you get a plain summary of what was said, and it takes no action beyond what you signed off on — no bookings, no payments, no cancellations. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
