## What it does

Once a week it runs a reverse-image search across a photographer's portfolio to see where those photos have surfaced online. Uncredited use and commercial use both get flagged, and for each finding it drafts a polite outreach message asking for proper credit. Drafts only — nothing is ever sent without you. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
