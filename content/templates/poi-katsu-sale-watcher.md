## What it does

Point it at the wishlists you keep on Japan's big shopping sites and it tracks each item against a target price that counts point-campaign multipliers, not just the sticker figure. You hear about a real discount or a major sale window and nothing else. It never checks out or pays; buying stays yours. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
