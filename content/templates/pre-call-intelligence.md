## What it does

Ahead of an important meeting it reads your calendar, CRM history, mail, chat threads and earlier call notes, then compresses all of it into a single card about the other side: what they care about, what is unresolved, what not to re-ask. After the call it drafts the follow-up and holds it, unsent, for your edits. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
