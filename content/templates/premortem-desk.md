## What it does

Hand it a plan you have already committed to and it works backwards from failure: the most likely ways things go wrong, sorted, each paired with an early warning sign worth watching. It also names the point where pivoting or stopping makes sense. An optional weekly check-in flags when one of those triggers has fired. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
