## What it does

Paste in links to the things you are waiting on and it re-checks each one's price every morning. When something reaches the number you named, or falls further than it ever has, you hear about it then instead of finding out the sale ended. It is watch-only: it never buys on its own, so the purchase stays your decision. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
