## What it does

Treat it like the attendant at a print shop. Ask for a part and it either tracks down a suitable model or drafts one, slices it for your specific machine, and kicks off the job after its safety checks pass. When a print fails halfway it stays on call, reads what went wrong and walks you through the fix. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
