## What it does

Hand it the email addresses and phone number you want out of circulation and it works through the categories that publish them: people-search sites, caller-ID lookups, email directories, business contact lists and marketing databases. You choose how often it repeats, because removed listings tend to reappear. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
