## What it does

Every day it works through Korean product-trial and review-campaign boards, including Musinsa and Kakao Gift, and applies to the ones matching the conditions you set. When the draws settle it reports back which ones you won. Handy for chasing review samples without watching the boards yourself. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
