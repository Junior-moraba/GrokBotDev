## What it does

Pick a subject and it teaches in small steps, checking understanding as it goes instead of lecturing straight through. The quiz loop is the point: it keeps asking until you can state the idea in your own words, where most self-study falls apart. It also keeps a reading list drawn from interests you actually hold. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
