## What it does

A pocket personal trainer that reads your daily activity numbers — calories, steps, distance covered and minutes exercised — and reshapes them into a compact report card. The output is formatted to screenshot cleanly, so posting your day somewhere or sending it to a friend takes one tap. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
