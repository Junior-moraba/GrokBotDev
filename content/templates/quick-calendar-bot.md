## What it does

You describe a plan the way you would say it out loud and it lands on your calendar correctly dated and timed. Reminders arrive when they should, and moving things is a conversation, not a form: state the change and it reshuffles the affected events. Built for people who abandon calendar apps over entry friction. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
