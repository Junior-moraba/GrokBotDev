## What it does

It adds up what your bot collection, its scheduled routines and your command-line coding agents consume, and shows the day's burn as a single figure. Alerts point at the specific thing driving the spend rather than just announcing that the total is high, so you throttle the right culprit. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
