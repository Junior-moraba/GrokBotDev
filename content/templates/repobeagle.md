## What it does

Paste a repository link before you clone anything and it reads the code to answer three questions: what the project does, what it touches on your machine and network, and what looks risky or unexplained. The verdict arrives in plain language, not a wall of flags. A pre-install sanity check for tools found online. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @jvr0x shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
