## What it does

Clearing out the house gets easier here: photograph what you want gone and it writes up draft listings for eBay and Facebook Marketplace, title to price. Every draft waits in a holding area until you approve it, and only then does it go live. Nothing is posted without your explicit sign-off. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
