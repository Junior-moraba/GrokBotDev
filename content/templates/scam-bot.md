## What it does

Defensive tooling for your X presence: it hunts down copycat accounts pretending to be you and files impersonation reports against them, spaced out in measured batches rather than all at once. It also gives a second opinion when a message reaches you and you are not sure whether it is a scam. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
