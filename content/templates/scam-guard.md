## What it does

Connects to a Gmail or Outlook inbox and walks it twice a day, marking messages that look like phishing or common fraud patterns. Each flag comes with a short reason so you can judge for yourself. It only applies labels: it will not delete, move or answer anything, and it never declares a message safe. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
