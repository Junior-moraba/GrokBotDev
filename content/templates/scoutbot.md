## What it does

Turns the chat into a dispatch desk for the developer tools on your machine: describe a job and it goes to whichever local coding agent suits it, whether Claude Code, Codex or something else you have installed. Results come back into the same conversation, so you never bounce between terminals to collect an answer. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @arach shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
