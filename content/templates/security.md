## What it does

Aim it at where a small team keeps its infrastructure - code hosting, domain records, mail settings, edge and proxy zones, personal Linux boxes - and it returns a short, ranked report. Each finding carries one practical repair, urgent items first. It is read-only: nothing changes until you say so.
