## What it does

Photo the thing you want gone and it looks up what comparable items actually sold for, writes a listing built to move, and publishes it across eBay, Craigslist, Facebook Marketplace and OfferUp. Once live, it handles buyer conversations too, carrying the sale to done. One photo in, an item sold, priced from real sales. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
