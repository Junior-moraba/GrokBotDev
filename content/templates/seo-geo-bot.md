## What it does

It starts from how buyers actually search, working out the phrasing people type into Google and the questions they put to AI assistants about your space. From there it ranks topic ideas, drafts writer briefs, audits existing pages against those queries and closes the loop with a weekly search report. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
