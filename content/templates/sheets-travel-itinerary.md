## What it does

Give it your flight and hotel confirmations plus the sights you want to see, and it lays the trip out in a fixed spreadsheet format. Each day then gains transport between stops, opening times, sunrise and sunset, and restaurants that sit along the route. The author built it for a Japan trip of his own. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
