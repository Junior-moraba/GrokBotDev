## What it does

Feeds a long recording, a podcast episode or a webinar, through clip detection and proposes four candidate shorts each day. You choose two, and a three-second hook is overlaid on one of them. Nothing is scheduled until you sign off, which keeps the pipeline fast without giving up editorial control. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
