## What it does

Give it a public web address and it loads the page at two viewport sizes, captures screenshots, and looks for horizontal overflow, dead links and errors in the browser console. A couple of minutes later you get the evidence plus a list of problems ranked by severity. A fast pre-launch pass for small sites. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
