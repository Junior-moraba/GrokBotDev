## What it does

A small-chore assistant for Slack, made for the work that sits outside development. Add a robot emoji to any message and it prepares three moves from the message's context: a task entry, a draft reply, a calendar slot. You pick one and it carries through, so handing off the errand takes a couple of clicks. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
