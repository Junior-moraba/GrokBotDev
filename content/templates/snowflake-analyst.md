## What it does

Sits read-only against a Snowflake warehouse so anyone can ask data questions in plain language. Each answer shows the query behind it and labels every figure with its source, and it weighs the cost of a heavy scan before running it. Packaged routines cover cohorts, experiment readouts, spend reviews and data checks. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
