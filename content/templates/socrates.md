## What it does

Bring a half-formed idea and it first restates it in the strongest version it can construct, steel-manning your position before it pushes back. Then the questions begin and keep coming until the parts that would not survive a sceptic are visible to you. It never simply agrees, which is the entire point. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
