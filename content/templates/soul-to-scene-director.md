## What it does

Work on a song with it and the additions keep your phrasing instead of drifting into somebody else's style. Once the lyrics hold together it plans a music video around them, with timed shots built from your own photos and recollections, so the finished piece still feels like it came from the person who wrote it. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
