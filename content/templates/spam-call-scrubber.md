## What it does

It searches the people-search and data-broker sites that publish your phone number, submits the removal requests, and works through the confirmation emails those sites send back, the step most attempts stall at. It then looks again on whatever cadence you set, because removed listings have a habit of reappearing. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
