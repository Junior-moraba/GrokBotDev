## What it does

Point it at your profile and it studies which companies already pay for sponsorships in your space - the ones with a record of backing fighters, athletes, newsletter writers and similar creators. It finds a working contact route and drafts a pitch for each brand. You press send yourself; nothing goes out on its own.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @MichaelHeckert shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
