## What it does

Sits at the front of a creator's sponsorship pipeline. Routine rate questions get an immediate first reply locked to a fixed price floor, while anything unusual, odd structure or brand conflict included, is held for a human. The script exists so terms stay consistent, and edge cases never get improvised on your behalf. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
