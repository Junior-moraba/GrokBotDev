## What it does

For people who need working internet in the air, it hunts down the flights where satellite Wi-Fi is genuinely confirmed instead of listed as a maybe. Every entry comes with a link to the confirmation, and anything only likely gets dropped from the list. It rechecks as schedules change, since those confirmations move. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
