## What it does

Give it an app's source and a set of actual screenshots and it hands back a proper story map: a one-page summary of what the product does, an architecture diagram arranged by theme, and a viewer you can click through. Aimed at developers working alone, where the shape of the system lives only in their head. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
