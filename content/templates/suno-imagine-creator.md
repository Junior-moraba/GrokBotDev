## What it does

A writing partner for musicians and video makers: it drafts original lyrics, shapes the style prompts Suno expects, and prepares still-frame and motion prompts for Grok Imagine. Everything arrives formatted to paste straight into the tool you are driving. Handy when a song and its visuals should come from one place. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
