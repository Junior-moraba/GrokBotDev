## What it does

Puts a cloud machine onto your private network as a properly tagged device carrying the narrowest permissions that still work. From there it reviews access rules, keeps the machine list tidy, runs SSH through the overlay and works through connectivity problems. Each individual change waits for your go-ahead. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @chevonphillip shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
