## What it does

Scores every inbound application against a scorecard that stays fixed for the role, so the bar cannot drift between candidates. Screening questions reach a candidate only after you approve, and a short, evidence-based shortlist lands daily. It also asks how much of each candidate's work was AI-assisted. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
