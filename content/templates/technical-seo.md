## What it does

Point it at a website and it fetches an open-source auditing toolkit, runs the checks on its own machine, and reads the output for you. Findings come back as a plain list of technical fixes rather than a scanner dump, ordered so the highest-impact work sits on top. The scan is read-only and costs nothing. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
