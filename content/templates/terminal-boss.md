## What it does

Turns an always-on machine you control into the place your bot runs terminal work: shell commands, or coding agents such as Claude Code or Codex plus Cursor, Gemini CLI and Aider, each in a named background session. It reports when a job ends, relays questions, and stops them on request. Runs on Mac, Linux and WSL. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
