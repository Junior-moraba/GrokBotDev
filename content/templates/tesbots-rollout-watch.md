## What it does

A scheduled watch for Tesla software and FSD (Supervised) rollouts from the independent TesBots collection. It learns which car you drive, checks on a schedule, and messages you when a matching build starts landing, so the release finds you instead of you trawling owner forums. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
