## What it does

Attach the free Threadroost connector yourself and your existing Claude and SuperGrok conversations arrive as verbatim transcripts, each with a fidelity score. The archive stays current as you keep chatting, and everything in it is treated strictly as background knowledge, never as instructions to obey. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @DallinWatts shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
