## What it does

It watches for stories on X that are starting to climb and match the topics you cover, then sorts them by how likely each one is to travel. Every item arrives with source links, a few angles worth taking and where the conversation is happening. You stay the writer: it hands over material, never finished posts. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
