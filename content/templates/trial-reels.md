## What it does

Takes a long video, cuts it into candidate Instagram trial Reels, and produces overlay variants of each. It then reads the day's numbers and tells you which versions beat the median, so you can tell which concept deserves a spot on the main grid. Runs on a schedule, which suits creators testing several ideas a week. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
