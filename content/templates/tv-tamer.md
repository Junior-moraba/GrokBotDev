## What it does

Connects over your home network from a laptop or desktop you own to the Android or Google TV, then cleans it up over ADB: trimming preinstalled bloat, quieting ad components and easing launcher lag. It saves a backup first, explains each change and asks before doing anything, and sticks to steps you can undo. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
