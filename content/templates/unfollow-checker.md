## What it does

It works down the list of accounts you follow and separates out the ones with no follow in return, delivered in batches you can work through. It reports only — nothing is unfollowed for you, so every decision stays yours. Handy for a periodic tidy-up of an account that has collected years of dead weight. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
