## What it does

Point your assistants at a single shared AGENTS folder and it becomes the one source of truth for all of them. Say the phrase and new facts get folded in, so every helper, whichever company built it, works from the same updated context next time. It ends the drift where each bot remembers a different version of you. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
