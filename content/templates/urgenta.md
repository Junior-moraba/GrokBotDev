## What it does

Watches a team's Slack channels and mail for signs that a system is down or a part is missing, then pulls in whoever is on call for that area right away. It checks back on a fixed rhythm, nudging twice a day until someone confirms the issue is closed. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
