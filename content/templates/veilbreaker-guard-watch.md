## What it does

Screens what your bot is about to consume, from web pages and mail to documents and tool results, hunting for hidden instructions and tricks that try to make it leak data. It keeps a running digest, runs self-tests on a schedule, and stops for your input whenever something looks off. Requires the companion plugin. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
