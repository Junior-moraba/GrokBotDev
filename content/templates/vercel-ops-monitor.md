## What it does

Each day it reviews a deployment on Vercel: how spend is tracking against plan credits, whether server errors spiked, how traffic is moving, which routes are missing and whether the latest deploys broke anything. A quiet day costs a single line, so the report stays readable even though it arrives daily. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
