## What it does

Point it at an app you assembled quickly with AI help and it hunts what weekend work forgets: sign-in and permissions, database wiring, payment handling, deployment basics, dependencies that do not exist, and keys left somewhere exposed. You get a ranked fix list, plus a quick top-ten pass for highlights. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
