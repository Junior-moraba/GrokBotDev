## What it does

Hand it a completed song and you get two visuals back: a vertical Short with the words timed to the music, plus a longer video that carries the whole track. Every step uses no-cost tools, so an independent or AI-music artist can give a release a real video without a production budget. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
