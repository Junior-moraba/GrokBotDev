## What it does

Hand it the footage from a filming session and it works like a showrunner. It decides what this installment is about, structures the cut around your strongest material, then hands back a finished edit with clean sound and subtitles. Aimed at creators who shoot on a phone and want a dependable editing partner. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
