## What it does

Made for customers of the Argentine fintech who want quick answers about their own account: where a payment went, whether a transfer cleared, how the holdings look. It states plainly that it has no affiliation with the company and gives no financial advice. Nothing moves unless you confirm each step. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @rodrigouroz shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
