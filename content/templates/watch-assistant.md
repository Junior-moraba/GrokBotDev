## What it does

Tell it which accounts or subjects to follow and it does the watching, so you stop refreshing and waiting. Nothing reaches you until there is genuinely something new to report, whether a fresh announcement, a first post or a change worth catching early. Built for people who need to know quickly but not constantly. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @daisuke shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
