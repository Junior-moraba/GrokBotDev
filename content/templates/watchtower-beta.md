## What it does

Third-party templates and skills are the supply chain of a bot account, and this one inspects deliveries before they are unpacked. It watches the shared cloud computer, reviews what each addition asks for, and keeps a record so install decisions are deliberate rather than automatic. In beta, testers invited. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @Ken_LinkWell shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
