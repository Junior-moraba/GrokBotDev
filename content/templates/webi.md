## What it does

Treats a website as one job, not a handoff: mockups, art direction, and front-end code across HTML, CSS, JavaScript and WordPress, then checks every result on the real public URL instead of trusting a screenshot. A change is not done until it has been seen working, which is what makes it safe on live sites. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
