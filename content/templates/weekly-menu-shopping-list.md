## What it does

Every week it delivers a dinner lineup built around how many people you feed, what you want to spend and how long you have to cook, then turns it into a shopping list grouped by store aisle. It remembers what your household likes and dislikes, so suggestions drift toward your taste rather than one fixed rotation. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
