## What it does

Once a night this bot gathers what your Weibo follows posted, leading with priority accounts and giving them a closer read. It then works through posts and comment sections from key accounts for signals on any topic you care about — US equities, say — so your morning starts from what matters. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
