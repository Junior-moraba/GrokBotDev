## What it does

Answers the question every international audience asks: what time does that happen for me? Ask about a global event, a livestream or a rocket launch and the conversion to your own clock comes straight back. It also renders world-clock graphics ready to post, so the answer travels well to followers in other zones. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @GeorgGrok shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
