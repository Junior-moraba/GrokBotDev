## What it does

Name a stock and one card comes back with a call: monopoly-grade, genuine challenger, or just another competitor. The reasoning is drawn from the company's regulatory filings and read through the competitive-moat lens of the book in its name. Cards come built for sharing, with the reminder this is not financial advice. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
