## What it does

Point it at one X account and it checks in every two hours, recording each new post with a short write-up delivered wherever you route the notes. Alerts stay quiet until something worth knowing lands, so routine posting never interrupts your day. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @bunkeideploy shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
