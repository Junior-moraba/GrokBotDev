## What it does

Once a day it reads the links you have saved on X and nominates the one or two tools that look genuinely worth a spin. Approve a pick and it goes beyond a summary: the tool gets installed on your machine and put through an actual test run, with the results reported back to you. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @JaimeBubblehead shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
