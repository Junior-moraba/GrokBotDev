## What it does

Each morning it goes through what you saved on X, pulls out the items that could become something, and ranks them by how likely the idea is to land with an audience. For the strongest it drafts a three-line summary plus a short-video script to shoot from. Nothing gets posted; every draft waits for your decision. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.
