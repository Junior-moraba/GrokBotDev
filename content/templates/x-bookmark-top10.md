## What it does

Name a subject and every morning you get the ten posts that were bookmarked most often on it over the past day, each reduced to a one-line summary with its link. Anything that smells like a scam is flagged so you do not click blind. It reads what is public and needs no X login of its own. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @Hmountain5 shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
