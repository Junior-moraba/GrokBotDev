## What it does

Once a day this reader goes through what you've saved and liked on X plus your For You feed, and turns it into a themed roundup with a chart and a quality score attached to each outside link. It stays strictly read-only — nothing is posted or liked on your behalf. A low-effort way to catch up on your saves and feed. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @genxtraders shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
