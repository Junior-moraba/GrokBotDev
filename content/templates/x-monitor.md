## What it does

Point it at an account and it reads the replies, sorting commenters into groups by attitude. Hostile ones get pulled out with follower counts attached, so you can see which criticism has real reach behind it. Replies that look like rule breaches are itemized, and everything lands in a spreadsheet refreshed weekly. It runs on a schedule rather than waiting to be asked.

## Before you install

Open the share link and read the instructions it carries before you add it. @BoKuangyi shared this one publicly, and adding it copies the setup into your own account rather than giving you access to theirs.
