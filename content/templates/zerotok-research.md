## What it does

Competitive research for storefronts on Shopify: hand over a domain and a report comes back covering estimated revenue, traffic levels, which products sell best, plus the advertising currently running. It is a quick read on how a rival store is doing, without assembling the picture from several dashboards yourself. It runs when you ask it to, rather than on a timer.
